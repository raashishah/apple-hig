# Brand interview (gate)

Max ~12 questions total across rounds. Confirm before writing DESIGN.md.

Required fields to collect:

- register: product | brand
- platform_primary: phone | ipad | desktop | multi
- users_and_scene: one sentence (who, where, light, mood)
- personality: 3 words
- accent_and_palette: free text or token list
- fonts: free text
- anti_references: list
- materials: opaque-chrome-glass-overlays (default) | custom note

Stop and wait for answers between rounds.
