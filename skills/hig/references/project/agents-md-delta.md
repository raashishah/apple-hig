## Apple HIG (web)

- Use `/hig` for teach / craft / review / adapt.
- `DESIGN.md` owns brand colors and fonts; HIG packs own structure and patterns.
- Materials: opaque nav and content chrome; glass only on functional overlays with `@supports` fallback.
- NavigationSplitView list columns are browsers (compact toolbar, dense rows); detail owns large title.
- Live web gold QA viewports: 768 and 375 unless the project says otherwise.
