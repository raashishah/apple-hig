# tech-researchkit

**Apple:** [ResearchKit](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/researchkit)  
**Gate:** `capability:researchkit` on surface `researchkit`. Unknown hosts skip. Skip-unless affordance `researchkit` (`data-researchkit`). `import ResearchKit` is the capability, not the widget. CareKit advertising stays on surface `carekit`. Do not complete Health permission or informed consent.

A research app lets people join a study. Ask only for data the study needs.

## Do

- Explain why the study needs location, Health, or other data.
- Determine eligibility before consent.

## Don't

- Requesting access to data that isn't critical to the study.
