# tech-carekit

**Apple:** [CareKit](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/carekit)  
**Gate:** `capability:carekit` on surface `carekit`. Unknown hosts skip. Skip-unless affordance `carekit` (`data-carekit`). `import CareKit` is the capability, not the widget. ResearchKit critical-data Don'ts stay on surface `researchkit`. The health-research cluster still launches for either capability. Do not complete Health permission.

People use CareKit apps to reach a health goal. Advertising distracts them from the care plan. The care symbol is about health, not a corporate logo or a decoration. Do not remove the symbol.

## Do

- Keep the care plan as the content.
- Let brand show through color and communication style.

## Don't

- Advertising in a care plan.
- A corporate logo or purely decorative symbol used as the care symbol.
