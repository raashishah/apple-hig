# Text fields

### text-fields.001
Use a text field for a short fact, for instance someone’s name or email. Longer passages belong in a control made for extended text.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.002
A hint inside the field can explain its purpose. Placeholder copy such as “Email” or “Password” appears only while the field is empty and vanishes once typing starts, so a separate label is also useful as a reminder.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.003
Hide private data with a secure text field. Always choose that field when the app asks for something sensitive, such as a password.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.004
Match the field’s size to how much text you expect, as far as you can. The dimensions help people judge how much to enter.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.005
Space several text fields evenly. Leave enough room that each introductory label clearly belongs with its field; stack the fields vertically when you can, and keep widths consistent so the layout stays organized. On an address form, for example, first and last name might share one width while address and city share another.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.006
Tab order should match what people expect, advancing focus logically. The system usually handles this, so you seldom need to customize it.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.007
Validate when the context calls for it. If a field may contain only digits, alert people who enter other characters. Check an email address when focus leaves the field, but check a user name or password before focus moves on.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.008
Use a number formatter for numeric entry. It accepts only numeric values and can show a chosen count of decimal places, a percent, or money. Formatting varies a lot by locale, so do not assume one presentation.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.009
Fit line breaking to the field. Anything past the bounds is clipped unless you change that; alternatives are wrapping on a character or a word, or an ellipsis at the start, the middle, or the end.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.010
An expansion tooltip can reveal the full text when copy is clipped or truncated. It behaves like a standard tooltip and appears when the pointer rests on the field.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.011
Show a keyboard suited to the input on iOS and iPadOS, and on tvOS and visionOS as well. Keyboards differ by entry type, including numbers and URLs, so pick the one that matches the content.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.012
Keep typing short for tvOS and for watchOS. Long passages or many fields are slow on Apple TV, and likewise on Apple Watch, so prefer controls such as buttons when you can.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.013
On iOS and iPadOS, a Clear button at the trailing end lets people erase input with one tap instead of repeated presses of the Delete key.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.014
Images and buttons can clarify a field and extend what it does. Custom artwork can occupy either end, and a system button such as Bookmarks is another option. Purpose usually belongs at the leading end; extras such as bookmarking belong at the trailing end.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.015
On macOS, consider a combo box when text entry needs to sit with a list of choices.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.016
On watchOS, show a text field only when you must. Prefer a list of options over requiring someone to type.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields

### text-fields.017
On June 5, 2023, the guidance was brought in line with watchOS 10.
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields
